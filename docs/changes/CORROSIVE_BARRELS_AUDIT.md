# Corrosive Barrels v2 source audit

Starting revision `06c786b614ff403ff43e68034396ce702f0f8b32` already contains v1. The v1 patch was therefore not reapplied. The baseline source and both original reports were reviewed locally.

| Area | Finding and correction |
| --- | --- |
| Explosion and damage | `A_Explode` has one unconditional stock `P_RadiusAttack(..., 128)` after the optional barrel hooks. That ordering and the stock source pointer are preserved. The acid hook independently deduplicates a rupture for the barrel's current lifetime. Leakage records only actual, nonlethal health loss after normal damage processing. |
| Damage identity | v1 used process-wide damage depth and still entered weak-point and aggression hooks before checking it. A dedicated acid entry point now uses a per-call flag in the shared damage routine. Armor, god mode, invulnerability, death and attacker credit use the normal route. Acid skips weapon impact, weak-point adjustment, retaliation, severe bloodspill and elemental confirmation. Nested ordinary damage keeps its own identity. |
| Victims and stacking | The v1 kill-count filter missed Lost Souls. Living, shootable players, Lost Souls, kill-count monsters and actors with native chase and attack states are eligible. Missiles, barrels, corpses and the hanging Commander Keen switch actor are excluded. A single exposure record per actor preserves the damage schedule when the actor contacts several pools or another rupture. |
| Spatial contact | The original center-sector comparison missed actors straddling a boundary. The new check combines actor and pool radii, floor height, sector footprint and low-height visibility. Spill satellites require an open route from the barrel. Airborne splash remains a separate LOS and three-dimensional range check. |
| Actor and map lifetime | Actor removal erases exposure and rupture bookkeeping, clears source references, and converts an orphaned leak into a puddle with its existing expiry. A removed thinker is guarded before and after the acid tick. Map setup clears acid state, and a live leak periodically follows its owner. |
| Save compatibility | Doom archives native actors in thinker order and reconstructs them in that same order before reading extensions. The writer emits weak-point, acid, Boom and Encounter Director trailers in that order. The reader peels the latter three in reverse order before restoring weak points. AC01 is restricted to bytes after the archived base, validates its framing and record counts, validates sectors and actor IDs, and skips a valid frame when disabled. The record layout is unchanged. |
| Rendering | V1 shared the glowing-eyes mode in world batches. The world shader guarded sprites, but the emission pass also visited those batches and tested that bit. V2 puts acid in the existing depth-tested alpha effect pass with a private procedural texture and plain vertex modes. World, shadow, depth prepass, eye emission and the camera uniform ABI no longer depend on acid flags. |
| Settings | The F4 Combat setting uses the generic graphics config and defaults off. Disabling clears state and multiplayer or demo modes suppress it. The launcher catalogue's category heuristic had filed the option under Destruction. It now emits Combat. |

The stock barrel sprite and stock explosion remain in use. Acid adds tinted surface shimmer, bubbles, leak drops and short airborne spray in the accelerated main view. It does not cast light into the room or appear in the separate mirror and emission passes. Pool meshes follow stock sector floor polygons and heights. Fractured solid-map floors, unusually authored slopes, mirrors and third-party actors without hostile flags or states need map-specific review. Standard DEH replacements inherit classification from their runtime type, flags and states.

The pool cap of 48 bounds per-actor candidate work. The thinker-list scan occurs on rupture and while serializing, not per actor each tic. Exposure storage grows with the number of currently or recently exposed actors. Release the feature only with the coverage described in the [test report](CORROSIVE_BARRELS_TEST_REPORT.md).
